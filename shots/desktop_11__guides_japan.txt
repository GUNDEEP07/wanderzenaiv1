Japan slow travel itinerary: three routes for 8–10 days

Fewer stops, longer stays. These three routes through Japan keep transfers short and leave whole days for walking, cafés and the day trips worth taking. Each one is a starting point: open it in the planner and it adjusts to your dates, pace and who's travelling.

Kyoto, the Alps and the Sea of Japan: 10 days

Temples at dawn in Kyoto, an old merchant town in the mountains, then gardens and seafood in Kanazawa.

When to go: Spring blossom and autumn colour; Jun–Jul is the rainy season.

Kyoto → Takayama → Kanazawa
Good with kids
Longest transfer ~3 h 30 min
Best Mar–May, Oct–Nov
1
DAYS 1–4
Kyoto

Quiet temples before 8am, northern hills, Philosopher’s Path.

Stay: machiya townhouse guesthouse

Day trips: Kurama & Kibune · Ohara · Nara

2
DAYS 5–7
Takayama · Gifu

Morning markets, Edo-era streets, Hida beef.

Stay: family-run ryokan

Getting there: Train, about 3 h 30 min. Via Nagoya on the scenic Hida line

Day trips: Shirakawa-go · Hida-Furukawa

3
DAYS 8–10
Kanazawa · Ishikawa

Kenroku-en, geisha districts, Omicho market.

Stay: small inn in Higashi Chaya

Getting there: Bus, about 2 h 15 min

Day trips: Noto peninsula · Kaga onsen

Tokyo, Hakone and Kyoto: 9 days

A gentle first trip: Tokyo neighbourhoods, a hot-spring night in Hakone, then Kyoto.

When to go: Spring blossom and autumn colour; Fuji is clearest Nov–Feb.

Tokyo → Hakone → Kyoto
Good with kids
Longest transfer ~2 h 30 min
Best Mar–May, Oct–Nov
1
DAYS 1–3
Tokyo

Yanaka and Kichijoji over Shibuya; parks and small museums.

Stay: small hotel in Yanaka or Kuramae

Day trips: Kamakura · Mt Takao

2
DAYS 4–5
Hakone · Kanagawa

Onsen, lake, ropeway views of Fuji on clear days.

Stay: ryokan with a private bath

Getting there: Train, about 1 h 30 min. Romancecar from Shinjuku

Day trips: Lake Ashi loop · Old Tokaido road walk

3
DAYS 6–9
Kyoto

Temples at dawn, Arashiyama away from the crowds.

Stay: machiya townhouse guesthouse

Getting there: Train, about 2 h 30 min. Odawara → Kyoto by shinkansen

Day trips: Nara · Uji

Kyushu hot-spring towns: 8 days

Fewer tourists: Fukuoka food stalls, the onsen village of Yufuin and Kurokawa’s riverside baths.

When to go: Onsen towns are at their best in cool weather; Jun–Jul is the rainy season.

Fukuoka → Yufuin → Kurokawa Onsen
Good with kids
Longest transfer ~2 h 15 min
Best Mar–May, Oct–Dec
1
DAYS 1–2
Fukuoka

Yatai food stalls, easy airport, Ohori park.

Stay: small hotel in Tenjin

Day trips: Dazaifu

2
DAYS 3–5
Yufuin · Oita

Lake Kinrin mist at dawn, private onsen.

Stay: ryokan with an open-air bath

Getting there: Train, about 2 h 15 min. Yufuin no Mori limited express

Day trips: Beppu hells

3
DAYS 6–8
Kurokawa Onsen · Kumamoto

Riverside bath-hopping in a village with no neon.

Stay: traditional ryokan

Getting there: Bus, about 2 h

Day trips: Mt Aso caldera
